## Project Overview

This project focuses on exploring and analyzing a hypothetical logistics dataset using Python.

The main purpose of this task is to understand logistics performance through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, and data visualization.

The analysis focuses on shipment volume, transportation distance, delivery time, delivery delays, transportation cost, customer satisfaction, transport modes, and regional performance.

---

## Objectives

- Create and explore a hypothetical logistics dataset.
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Calculate mean, median, and standard deviation.
- Analyze distributions of logistics variables.
- Study shipment volume trends.
- Compare different transportation modes.
- Analyze the relationship between delivery time and transportation cost.
- Perform correlation analysis.
- Compare logistics performance across regions.
- Identify potential bottlenecks and cost drivers.
- Provide data-driven recommendations.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis

The dataset was checked for:

- Dataset shape
- Data types
- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Statistical summary

### 2. Central Tendency Analysis

The following measures were calculated:

- Mean
- Median
- Standard deviation

These were calculated for the main numerical logistics variables.

### 3. Delivery Time Analysis

A histogram was created to understand the distribution of delivery times and identify variations in delivery performance.

### 4. Delivery Delay Analysis

The distribution of delivery delays was analyzed to understand delayed shipments and delivery reliability.

### 5. Transportation Cost Analysis

A histogram was used to study the distribution of transportation costs and identify high-cost observations.

### 6. Shipment Volume Trend

A line chart was used to analyze shipment volume over time.

### 7. Transport Mode Analysis

Average delivery time and average transportation cost were compared across:

- Road
- Rail
- Air

### 8. Delivery Time vs Transportation Cost

A scatter plot was created to study the relationship between delivery time and transportation cost.

### 9. Correlation Analysis

A correlation matrix and heatmap were created to study relationships between numerical variables.

### 10. Regional Analysis

Logistics performance was compared across different regions using:

- Shipment volume
- Delivery time
- Delivery delay
- Transportation cost
- Customer satisfaction
